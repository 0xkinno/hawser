# Not modelled, so not claimed

The engine counts minutes under the rules below and nothing else. A result that depends on any clause listed here is outside what HAWSER claims.

Modelled
- SHINC, SHEX, SSHEX (Saturday counts until a stated cut-off, otherwise not), port holidays, an optional Friday cut-off.
- Laytime commencing a fixed number of hours after NOR, or at a stated time on the next working day.
- Excepted stoppages by reason (for example weather), paired start to end.
- "Once on demurrage, always on demurrage", switchable per charter.
- Demurrage on time over the allowance. Despatch on laytime saved, at the despatch rate given.
- NOR validity: window, holiday, arrival, free pratique. The result is the engine's reading of the stated terms, not a legal conclusion.

Not modelled
- "Unless used" and "even if used" wording (SHEX unless used, EIU). Work done in an excepted period is not credited back. The external example `xshipx-ex1-shex-unless-used` is a recorded mismatch for this reason.
- Half-time-used-to-count rules.
- Reversible laytime across load and discharge ports. Each port is calculated alone.
- Despatch on all time saved (as opposed to laytime saved), and despatch rates other than the single rate given.
- Shifting time between berths, and any time-lost-in-waiting clause beyond what the stated commencement rule gives.
- Laytime allowed derived from cargo quantity and rate. The allowance is an input in minutes.
- Notice time that depends on whether the NOR was given before or after noon (the engine takes one commencement rule per charter).
- "Time actually used before commencement shall count".
- Weather working day proration beyond the stoppage periods supplied. The engine never decides whether weather would have stopped work.
- Strikes, ice, quarantine, port closures, and any clause not named under Modelled.
- Commissions, interest, and currency conversion.
- Waiver, estoppel and acceptance by conduct. The engine only knows the NOR decision a person recorded.
- Times that carry no zone are never placed. The reconciler abstains and asks.
